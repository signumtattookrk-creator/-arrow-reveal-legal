Frequently asked questions

How do I restore Remove Ads?

Open Shop or Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID that made the purchase. Restoration is free.

A line will not slide out. Is that a bug?

A line can leave only when its path is completely clear in the direction of its arrow. Rotate the shape, remove blocking lines first, or use a booster.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. Core levels, progress and the collection work offline. Ads, rewarded bonuses and purchase verification require an internet connection.

What does Remove Ads remove?

Remove Ads disables interstitial advertising. Rewarded videos remain optional and appear only when you choose to request a reward.

How do I manage advertising privacy choices?

Open Settings - Privacy Settings in the Game where this option is available. You can also manage tracking permission in iOS Settings - Privacy & Security - Tracking. See the Privacy Choices section.

Can deleted progress be recovered?

Progress is stored locally on the device and is not synced to an account. If the Game is deleted, local progress may not be recoverable. A verified Remove Ads purchase can still be restored.
